Make it yours




Firefox still manages passwords, downloads, history, site permissions and browser extensions.


Supports hostnames, block:, allow:, ||host^ and @@||host^. Full EasyList syntax and cosmetic filtering are not supported.


An exception allows requests from the site and to its subdomains. Reload a website after changing its rules.


Separate identities
Containers keep cookies separate where Firefox supports them. This does not hide your network identity.


Backup
Export includes saved page addresses and settings, but no passwords or cookies. Import adds saved pages and workspaces; it keeps your current settings.


Streaming & compatibility
Prime Video and other services open as ordinary Firefox tabs, using Firefox’s own codecs and DRM components. Aster does not bundle Widevine or change your browser identity.
On desktop, update Firefox and enable “Play DRM-controlled content” in Firefox Settings. If playback fails, turn Aster blocking off, reload the service, and check its supported browsers. Android services may require their official app. HD, 4K, offline downloads and every streaming service are not guaranteed.
